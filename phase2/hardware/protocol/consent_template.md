# Participant information and consent (template: get it approved by the guides first)

**Project:** Edge-Based Medical Device Self-Diagnosis System for Sensor Drift and Signal Degradation Detection
(final-year project, Department of ECE, New Horizon College of Engineering, Bengaluru).
**Guides:** TVS Adinarayana, Shasikiran S.

**What happens.** You sit still for about 15 minutes with a small light sensor (a MAX30102 pulse sensor, the
kind used in fitness bands) clipped to one fingertip. At times we will ask you to tap your finger, move your arm
slowly or let us flick the sensor cable, and the sensor's light will be dimmed by software. Optionally a fingertip
pulse oximeter is placed on the other hand.

**Risks.** The sensor uses the same low-power red and infrared light as consumer pulse oximeters. There is no
needle, no electrical contact with the body and no medical decision based on these readings. You may stop at any
time without giving a reason.

**Your data.** We record only the light signal, the sensor's own health numbers and the reference heart rate.
No name, photo or contact detail is stored with the data; you are only a code (for example S03). Recordings are
kept encrypted on the project laptop and Raspberry Pi, shared with the four project members by private link, and
never uploaded to public code websites. Results are published only as group statistics.
